Process & Investment

How We Build Your 3D Model

From photos and measurements to an interactive 3D model you can explore and iterate on. Here's how it works and what it costs.

The Process

1
You Send Photos & Measurements

Photograph your space from multiple angles. Provide basic dimensions and measurements. Tell us about your design goals, material preferences, and what you're trying to achieve. We ask follow-up questions to get it right.

Your time investment: 30–45 minutes

2
We Build the 3D Model

Our team creates a detailed 3D model of your space. We capture architectural details, proportions, existing finishes, and every element that makes your space unique. The result is an accurate, navigable 3D environment you can explore from every angle.

Our timeline: 3–5 business days

3
You Explore & Iterate

You get an interactive 3D model you can explore in real time. Click, drag, zoom — walk through your space. Toggle materials, colors, layouts, and design options instantly. Share feedback. We refine and adjust.

Your time investment: As much as you need

4
Finalize & Implement

Once you're confident in your design, use your 3D model to brief contractors, order materials with precision, and communicate your vision clearly. Your 3D model becomes the reference for the entire project — fewer surprises, fewer change orders, lower costs.

Results: Clarity, confidence, execution
